Rework of the single blocking finding in `TASK-260822-slgewd_review-verdict.md`.

## The blocking finding

`qwen/exec`'s `env_removed` covered 100% of its recorded `parent_env` — 18
seeded, 18 stripped, zero survivors. A port whose `ChildEnv` discards the whole
parent environment and returns only its injections therefore produced the
identical diff and byte-matched the golden through the real `agentic.BuildPlan`.
The golden proved `filterQwenRuntimeEnv`'s lower bound (strips at least these)
and was silent on its upper bound (strips no more) — the property the source
guards with its own `spawn_test.go:940` test.

## The fix

Four bystander keys added to `PINNED_ENV` in `.scripts/capture-parity-goldens.sh`,
then all 14 goldens regenerated by the same script at the same source commit.

| Key | What it pins |
|---|---|
| `PARITY_BYSTANDER` | a plain unrelated key every system must pass through |
| `CODEX_LIKE_BUT_NOT` | the `CODEX_*` strip is an exact key set, not a prefix match |
| `CLAUDECODE_LIKE_BUT_NOT` | the `CLAUDECODE` strip is an exact key, not a prefix |
| `TASK_BOARD_LIKE_BUT_NOT` | `appendOrReplaceEnv` replaces exact keys, not a prefix |

The exact-key claim is the source's own: `filterEnvKeys` builds a blocked-KEY
map (`spawn.go:998`) and `appendOrReplaceEnv` matches whole keys. A port
reaching for `strings.HasPrefix("CODEX_")` is a plausible defect that only a
near-miss can catch, which is why three of the four are near-misses.

| Test | Attack | Reported field |

- `and would not be caught by a plain bystander alone` — strips only the three
  near-misses and asserts the prefix defect produces **zero** differences. That
  is what earns the three near-misses their place rather than one plain key.
